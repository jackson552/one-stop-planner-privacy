One Stop Planner (Unofficial) — Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: One Stop Planner reads pages you open while signed in to your university's own sites, and keeps what it reads only in your browser. It has no server. It doesn't send your information to the developer or anyone else, and it doesn't sell, share or track anything.

Who makes it

One Stop Planner is an unofficial project by an individual student. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Miami University.

What it reads

Only pages on these sites, and only in the session you're already signed in to:

  • uAchieve (uachieve.miamioh.edu): degree audit reports you open — program, catalog year, requirement statuses, courses applied to requirements, course history and grades shown on the audit — and which programs are declared.
  • Banner Student Registration (banss.miamioh.edu): registration status (holds, class standing, curriculum), your registration time when Banner shows it, the classes you're registered or waitlisted for this semester and next, and the public class schedule (sections, seats, meeting times, course attributes).
  • Course List (www.apps.miamioh.edu/courselist): course sections and descriptions, and your override requests when you open that page. When you click "Request override" for a full section, it fills in Course List's search to find that section for you.

It never reads or stores your password, and never signs in for you.

What it stores, and where

Everything is kept in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer: the audits and registration information above, your semester plans, notes you add with "Mark as covered", a copy of this semester's class schedule (so it still shows when Banner can't be reached), and cached class schedules. Pre-registration intents saved by earlier versions stay there until you delete saved data. It does not store your name or student ID.

What it sends

  • Requests go only to the university sites listed above, using your existing session, to load the pages and class schedule information you're viewing.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or any other server. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • "Copy diagnostic report" copies a description of how pages were read to your clipboard — without your name, ID, grades, GPA or courses taken. It's only shared if you paste it somewhere yourself.
  • "Register" opens your university's registration page and fills in course numbers; you submit there yourself.
  • "Request override" opens Course List and searches for the section; you submit any override request there yourself.

Your choices

  • Delete saved data at the bottom of the Degree audit page clears everything the extension stored.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome also deletes its stored data.

Use of data

Data is used only to show you your own degree progress, plans and registration information. It is not sold, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
